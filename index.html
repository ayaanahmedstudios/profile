Graphic Designer and Photographer.

I am currently studying Graphic Design at OCAD University, where my practice focuses on brand identity, editorial systems, typography, and visual storytelling. I gravitate toward work that feels tactile, structured, and intentional—whether that means building a visual system from the ground up, designing for print, or developing custom tools for the web.

Alongside design, photography and lens-based media are a constant part of how I observe and document the world. I care deeply about spatial balance, quiet details, and creating work that communicates clearly without unnecessary noise.
